The existing installed campaign launcher first validated each separately frozen characterization manifest, installed and used the actual primary engine, checked independently generated records and relationships, forced two reopens and checked the full corpus again. The 1,000-record case contained 5,000 edges and 1,000 384-dimensional vectors; the 10,000-record case contained 50,000 edges and 10,000 such vectors. Both acknowledged and independently verified every initial record and every post-reopen record, completed with exit 0 and confirmed cleanup. Each measured phase was only 20 seconds at one request per second and recorded 20 attempts with zero failures; both report `qualification_passed: false`. These are synthetic characterization inputs, not project indexing, nearest-neighbor recall or a competitive benchmark.

The 10,000-record launcher emitted four thresholded slow resource-acquisition traces: total times 231.6?317.5 ms, including diagnostic RPC times 214.6?296.0 ms. These are selected samples above 200 ms, not a latency distribution; RPC includes more than store lock time. One final-process storage observation in the 10,000 case showed a 4 MiB page cache, 200 segments and approximately 2.45 GB cumulative segment reads, compared with 16 segments and approximately 40 MB in the smaller case. Counters are process-local, reset at reopen, and do not establish causation or a matched throughput comparison. Further correlated server/store tracing is needed before claiming a bottleneck or tuning choice.
